When to stop

This walkthrough is for a short spell — the kind that interrupts a meal or a call.

It is not for hiccups that last a long time, keep coming back over days, or arrive with other symptoms. We will not name a number of hours and call it a rule. If you are unsure whether what you have is ordinary, that uncertainty is already a reason to ask a clinician rather than to refresh this page.

Stop the sequence and get medical help if hiccups come with trouble breathing, chest pain, vomiting, severe belly pain, confusion, or anything else that feels wrong. A website countdown cannot sort that out.

Repeating a home sequence will not make a lasting problem safer. Close the tab, tell someone what is going on, and use the care you would use for any other symptom that will not leave.

If what you have is the short kind, the walkthrough is on the home page.
