Method

Callie's sequence, written out

5 September 2026

The home page timer is there so you do not have to count in your head. This note is the same sequence in writing, if you would rather read first.

Sit or stand still. You do not need water, a spoon, or anyone to scare you.

First: take a deep breath in. Swallow.

Second: take another breath in on top of the first, as if you are stacking air. Swallow again. Do not dump the first breath to make room.

Third: hold. Thirty full seconds. The countdown on the home page is the clock we trust more than a silent count that tends to shrink.

Fourth: let the air out slowly, as through the tiniest straw you can imagine. Ten seconds of that thin stream. If you still have air and it stays gentle, keep going to twelve or fifteen seconds.

That is the whole method. We do not add extra steps, and we do not say it always works. If you feel lightheaded, stop and breathe normally.

Press Start when you want the page to call the beats for you.
