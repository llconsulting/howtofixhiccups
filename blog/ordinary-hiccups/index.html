Ordinary hiccups are usually brief

5 September 2026

Most hiccups people search for in the middle of dinner are short. They are annoying. They steal a sentence. They often leave on their own whether anyone does anything clever or not.

That is why this site stays small. We are not building a museum of sugar-spoon tricks, paper-bag stories, or surprise scares. Those lists already exist, and they tend to grow faster than they help.

Callie's method is one personal sequence for those ordinary, short-lived spells. It is written in four beats and paced by a countdown so the long hold is actually thirty seconds. It is not offered as a universal fix, and we will not invent a reason it “should” work.

If you came here because hiccups are rare for you and you just want them to hush so you can finish a call, start on the home page. If they have been hanging around, read when hiccups linger instead of repeating a home sequence out of stubbornness.
