The method

Do these four steps in this order.

  1. Breathe all the way in, then swallow that breath. Fill your lungs, swallow that breath, and wait until the swallow finishes.
  2. Take another breath on top of the first and swallow completely. Keep the first breath, add the second on top, and swallow all the way.
  3. Hold that breath for 30 full seconds. Keep the air in, stay still, and wait for the full 30 seconds.
  4. Blow out slowly, as if through the smallest straw, for 10 full seconds. Keep the stream thin, and continue to between 12 and 15 seconds if you can.

On this page

Sit or stand still, and press Start when you are ready.

Swallow before you take the next breath, hold until the count finishes, and then blow in a thin stream for the full count.

If you feel lightheaded, stop and breathe normally.

This page is for adults with ordinary hiccups. One calm pass is enough, and once more the same evening is fine for a short spell.

If hiccups are persistent, keep coming back, or show up with other symptoms, stop.

Common questions

Does this always work?

It does not always work. Ordinary hiccups often leave on their own, and the four steps are here if you want to try them.

Do I need water, sugar, or someone to scare me?

You do not. This method is a breath and a swallow, and water is optional.

Will they stop in 30 seconds?

Thirty seconds is the hold on step 3. This page does not promise that hiccups stop at 30 seconds.

Is holding my breath enough?

The hold is step 3. Do the two swallows first, because this is one four-step method.

Can I use this for a baby or a pet?

This page is for adults with ordinary hiccups, not babies or pets.

Can I try after a meal or a drink?

Yes, if you feel safe to sit or stand still and breathe.

If you feel dizzy or unsafe, stop.

If hiccups are persistent, read When to stop.

What if I lose the first breath on step 2?

Start over from step 1. Keep the first breath, add the second on top, and swallow completely.

What if I feel dizzy?

Stop, breathe normally, and sit down if you need to.

How many times can I run it?

One calm pass is enough, and once more the same evening is fine for a short spell. If hiccups are persistent, stop and read When to stop.
