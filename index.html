The method

Four steps. This order.

  1. Deep breath all the way in and swallow it. Fill all the way. Swallow that breath. Wait until the swallow finishes.
  2. Another breath in on top of that and swallow completely. Keep the first breath. Add the second on top. Swallow all the way.
  3. Hold breath 30 full seconds. Keep the air in. Stay still. Wait for the full 30.
  4. Slowly blow out as through the tiniest straw for 10 full seconds. Go to 12 to 15 if you can. Thin stream. Like the tiniest straw. Stay on the count.

Common questions

Does this always work?

No.

Ordinary hiccups often leave on their own.

The four steps are here if you want to try them.

Do I need water, sugar, or someone to scare me?

No.

This method is breath and a swallow.

Water is optional.

Will they stop in 30 seconds?

Thirty seconds is the hold on step 3.

We do not promise they stop at 30 seconds.

Is holding my breath enough?

Hold is step 3.

Do the two swallows first.

This is one four-step method.

Can I use this for a baby or a pet?

This page is for adults with ordinary hiccups, not babies or pets.

Can I try after a meal or a drink?

Yes, if you feel safe to sit or stand still and breathe.

If you feel dizzy or unsafe, stop.

If the spell lasts, read When to stop.

What if I lose the first breath on step 2?

Start over from step 1.

Keep the first breath. Add the second on top.

Swallow completely.

What if I feel dizzy?

Stop.

Breathe normally.

Sit down if you need to.

How many times can I run it?

One calm pass.

Once more the same evening is fine for a short spell.

If it has been going for days, stop and read When to stop.
