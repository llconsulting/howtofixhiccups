The method

Do the steps in this order. Do not skip. Do not add extras.

  1. Deep breath all the way in and swallow it. Breathe all the way in. Swallow. Feel it finish before the next breath.
  2. Another breath in on top of that and swallow completely. Add the second breath on top. Swallow completely before you hold.
  3. Hold breath 30 full seconds. Hold until the count finishes. Stay still for all 30 seconds.
  4. Slowly blow out as through the tiniest straw for 10 full seconds. Go to 12 to 15 if you can. Blow thin, like the tiniest straw. Keep that stream going for the full count.

How to run it

Sit or stand still.

You do not need water.

You do not need a spoon.

You do not need anyone else.

This page is for adults with ordinary hiccups.

Common questions

Does this always work?

No.

There is no 100% method on this site.

The four steps are still here if you want to try them.

Ordinary hiccups often leave on their own.

Do I need water, sugar, or someone to scare me?

No.

This method is breath and a swallow only.

Water is optional.

Will they stop in 30 seconds?

Thirty seconds is the hold on step 3.

We do not promise they stop at 30 seconds.

Is holding my breath enough?

Hold is step 3.

Do the two swallows first.

This is one four-step method.

Can I use this for a baby or a pet?

This page is for adults with ordinary hiccups, not babies or pets.

Can I try after a meal or a drink?

Yes, if you feel safe to sit or stand still and breathe.

If you feel dizzy or unsafe, stop.

If the spell lasts, read When to stop.

What if I lose the first breath on step 2?

Start over from step 1.

Stack the second breath on top.

Swallow completely.

What if I feel dizzy?

Stop.

Breathe normally.

Sit down if you need to.

How many times can I run it?

One calm pass.

Once more the same evening is fine for a short spell.

If it has been going for days, stop and read When to stop.
