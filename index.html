How to fix hiccups

You have hiccups. This is one method. Four steps. You can do them sitting still.

A quiet sequence you can run in place. No water. No extra props.

Not medical advice. For ordinary, short-lived hiccups.

The method

Do the steps in this order. Do not skip. Do not add extras.

  1. Deep breath all the way in and swallow it. One full inhale. Swallow at the top of it.
  2. Another breath in on top of that and swallow completely. Stack the second breath. Do not dump the first one to make room.
  3. Hold breath 30 full seconds. Use the countdown. A count in your head tends to shrink.
  4. Slowly blow out as through the tiniest straw for 10 full seconds. Go to 12 to 15 if you can. A thin stream. Keep it gentle.

How to run it

Sit or stand still.

You do not need water.

You do not need a spoon.

You do not need anyone else.

Press Start if you want the page to keep time.

The hold is 30 full seconds.

The blow is 10 full seconds of a thin stream.

If you still have air and it stays gentle, keep that same thin stream going to 12 or 15 seconds.

If you feel lightheaded, stop and breathe normally.

If they are still going after one pass, you can try once more.

If they last, keep coming back, or come with other symptoms, stop.

Common questions

Do I need a drink or a spoon?

No.

Breath and a swallow.

That is the whole kit.

What if I lose the first breath on step 2?

Start over from step 1.

Stack the second breath on top.

Swallow completely.

What if I feel dizzy?

Stop.

Breathe normally.

Sit down if you need to.

Can I repeat it?

Once more is fine for a short spell.

Do not turn this into a loop.

Lasting or severe hiccups belong with a clinician.
