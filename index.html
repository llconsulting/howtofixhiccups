How to fix hiccups

Callie's method is four beats: breathe in and swallow, a second breath on top and swallow, hold 30 full seconds, then blow out as through the tiniest straw.

Not medical advice. For ordinary, short-lived hiccups only.

Callie's method

Ready when a hiccup shows up

Start guides the sequence: deep breath in and swallow, second breath on top and swallow, a 30-second hold, then a thin 10-second blow.

30 second hold ahead

The required blow is 10 seconds. Stay with a thin stream to 12–15 if you can.

The walkthrough

  1. Deep breath in, then swallow. One full inhale. Swallow at the top of it.
  2. Second breath on top, then swallow. Add another inhale without letting the first one go. Swallow again.
  3. Hold 30 full seconds. The on-page countdown keeps the hold honest. Do not cut it short.
  4. Blow as through the tiniest straw. A slow, thin stream for 10 seconds. If you can, keep that same thin stream going to 12–15 seconds.

What this is, and is not

This page walks one personal sequence Callie uses when ordinary hiccups interrupt a meal, a call, or sleep. It is not a clinic, not a diagnosis, and not a promise that hiccups will stop.

If hiccups last a long time, keep returning, or arrive with pain, trouble breathing, vomiting, or other symptoms, skip home tricks and talk to a clinician.
