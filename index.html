How to fix hiccups

Four beats. You can do them sitting still.

Not medical advice. For ordinary, short-lived hiccups only.

The sequence

Ready when you are

Start walks you through it. Two breaths with a swallow, a 30-second hold, then a thin blow.

30 second hold ahead

The required blow is 10 seconds. Stay with a thin stream to 12–15 if you can.

The four beats

  1. Deep breath in, then swallow. One full inhale. Swallow at the top of it.
  2. Second breath on top, then swallow. Add another inhale without letting the first one go. Swallow again.
  3. Hold 30 full seconds. The countdown keeps the hold honest. Do not cut it short.
  4. Blow as through the thinnest straw. A slow, thin stream for 10 seconds. If you can, keep that same thin stream going to 12–15 seconds.
