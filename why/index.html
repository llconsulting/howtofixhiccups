Why hiccups start

People get hiccups after ordinary moments. A fast meal. A fizzy drink. A laugh that hits while you swallow. A gulp of air you did not mean to take.

The usual story

You were eating or talking or laughing.

Then a hiccup showed up and stole the next sentence.

That is the kind this site is for.
