Why hiccups start

A hiccup is a sudden squeeze of the diaphragm, then a quick close of the vocal cords. That snap is the sound.

They often show up after a gulp of air, a fast meal, a fizzy drink, or a laugh. Most spells are short. They can leave on their own whether anyone does anything clever or not.

That is why this site stays small. It is not a museum of sugar-spoon tricks or surprise scares. Those lists already exist, and they tend to grow faster than they help.

The walkthrough on the home page is one sequence for those ordinary, short-lived spells. Four beats. A countdown so the long hold is actually thirty seconds. It is not offered as a universal fix, and we will not invent a reason it “should” work.

If you came here because a short spell interrupted a meal or a call, start the sequence. If they have been hanging around, read when to stop instead of repeating a home sequence out of stubbornness.
