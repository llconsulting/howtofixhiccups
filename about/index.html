About Callie's method

Callie is the person who showed this sequence to the people who put up this site. We wanted one calm place to try it — not a pile of folk lists, and not a lecture.

The method itself is short on purpose. Deep breath in, swallow. Second breath on top, swallow. Hold thirty full seconds. Blow out as through the tiniest straw for ten seconds, longer if a thin stream is still there.

howtofixhiccups.com is a static page with a countdown so the hold and the blow stay honest. There is no account, no course, and no product in the cart.

Nothing here is medical advice. We do not claim a cure, a success rate, or a scientific mechanism. Ordinary hiccups often pass on their own. Persistent or worrying hiccups belong with a clinician, not with a website timer.
